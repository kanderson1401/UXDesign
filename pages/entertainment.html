Entertainment

Enjoy yourself to the Max

Things to do!

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and to visit the volcano.Also, a nine-hole golf course should be operational by next year. Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, which is a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Thrill Seeking!

The island provides all kinds of different activities! Visitors can participate in zip-lining through the rainforest, take helicopter rides, swim with the exotic fish and more!

Aqua activities

Want to go on a chartered fish tour? Or go snorkeling with fish native to our island? We can make that happen! Our beaches provide multiple forms of entertainment for your vacation experience!

Touring

Most tourists spend most of their time in Taniti City, which boasts native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay. Other popular activities include boat or bus tours of the island, hikes in the rainforest, or visits to Taniti’s active volcano.

Other Activities

The island also offers other activities such as: movies, an arcade, art galleries, a museum for history lovers, bowling and soon to be, golf.
