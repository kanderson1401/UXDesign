The Stay

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts.

Tidy and regulated

All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government. We want to provide perfection and will be enforced to insire visitors have the best possible stay.
